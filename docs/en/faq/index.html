Arrival and departure

What time is check-in?

From 5 pm to 8 pm. On arrival, ring the “Reception” bell: Francesca or Janith will welcome you, hand over the keys and show you around the house. Early check-in is available on request.

What if we arrive after 8 pm?

No problem — just let us know in advance and we’ll arrange a late check-in.

What time is check-out?

Check-out is by 10 am on the day of departure.

Which address should I put in the GPS?

To drive in, use Via Sego de la Vecia 1, Custoza: it’s the one-way driveway that leads straight to our private parking. The official address, Via Chiesa 8, 37066 Custoza di Sommacampagna (VR), is used as the exit only. On arrival, please don’t stop in front of the photocell — the gate opens outwards.

Can we get there without a car?

Yes: Verona Airport is about 10 km away and Villafranca di Verona railway station about 7 km. From there, a taxi or a rental car is the easiest option.

Booking and registration

How many people does the house sleep?

Up to 12 guests, in 6 bedrooms with 6 bathrooms. The house is rented as a whole.

What is the minimum stay?

Two nights.

How does direct booking work?

Pick your dates on the calendar and send a request. Within 24 hours we confirm availability, price and payment details; until you confirm, the request is not binding.

Why do we need to register every guest?

Italian law requires all guests to be registered with the local authorities. Before arrival we’ll send you an online form to fill in for each traveller, with a copy of the main guest’s ID card or passport. Every guest needs to bring a valid ID.

Is there a tourist tax?

Yes, it’s the municipal tourist tax and it is paid on site.

The house and amenities

Is housekeeping included?

Yes. Janith and Imasha, the caretakers who live on the property in a separate private area, clean the house for two hours a day, included in the price. Extra hours can be arranged at an additional cost.

What is the pool like?

It’s a heated mini-pool with whirlpool jets, 98 cm deep. Children must be supervised by an adult at all times and diving is not allowed. We decline all responsibility for accidents resulting from unsupervised use.

Is there parking?

Yes, private and gated, for up to 3 cars. There is plenty of public parking just outside the gate.

Can I charge an electric car?

Yes, there is a charging station with two connectors in the car park.

Can we barbecue?

Yes, there is a gas barbecue in the garden for guests to use.

How does recycling work?

Please separate paper and cardboard, plastic, and glass and cans; the bins for general and organic waste are in the kitchen. Our staff takes care of the rest.

House rules

Is smoking allowed?

No, Il Metrone is a strictly non-smoking house.

Are pets allowed?

No. Out of respect for guests with allergies, pets are not allowed anywhere on the property.

Can we throw a party?

No, parties and crowded events are not permitted, to keep the house peaceful. Please observe quiet hours from 10 pm to 8 am, out of respect for our neighbours.

During your stay

Who do we ask if we need anything?

Janith, who is on site throughout your stay, is your first point of contact. For important matters that can’t be solved on the spot, you can contact Francesca, the owner.

Do you have tips for restaurants, wineries and things to do?

Yes, we’ve put together an interactive map with our favourite restaurants, wineries, golf courses and places to visit.

Emergency numbers?

Emergencies: 112. Medical emergencies: 118. The nearest hospital is in Villafranca di Verona, about 8 km away.

Book directly with us

Pick your dates on the calendar and send us a request: we reply within 24 hours with confirmation and a quote, with no booking fees.
