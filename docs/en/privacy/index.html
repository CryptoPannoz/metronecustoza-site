Privacy notice

Last updated: October 2026

Data controller

Il Metrone, Via Chiesa 8, 37066 Custoza di Sommacampagna (VR), Italy. Contact: metronecustoza@gmail.com.

What we collect

Through the request form we collect your name, email, phone number, dates of stay, number of guests and any message. We never ask for payment details or documents through the website.

Why we use it

Only to answer your request and, if you book, to manage your stay (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, pre-contractual and contractual measures). We do not use your data for newsletters or advertising.

How it is processed

The form sends your data to a Google Apps Script linked to our Google account: the request is saved in a private Google Sheet and reaches us by email, and you receive a summary email. Google acts as data processor. Your data is never sold or passed on to third parties.

How long we keep it

We keep requests for as long as needed to handle them; if you book, for the period required by tax and legal obligations.

Availability calendar

To show free dates, the booking page loads the list of booked nights from a Google server (script.google.com). As with any web request, Google receives your IP address.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies and no analytics or profiling tools. Fonts are hosted on our own server. If you close the language notice, your choice is remembered only in your browser (localStorage).

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or restrict your data, or object to its processing, by writing to metronecustoza@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian data protection authority (garanteprivacy.it).
